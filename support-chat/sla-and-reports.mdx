## SLA Policies

An SLA policy promises how fast conversations get a **first response** and how fast they get **resolved**. Define policies under **Settings → Support**; every policy needs at least one of the two targets.

- **Scope policies by priority and inbox.** A policy can apply to everything (a catch-all), to a priority level, or to a priority within a specific inbox.

- **The most specific policy wins.** For any conversation, a priority + inbox match beats a priority-only match, which beats the catch-all. There's no stacking — exactly one policy applies.

- **Badges appear before the miss.** Conversations approaching a target show an **SLA due** badge in the list; missed targets switch to a breached badge. Agents see trouble while it can still be fixed.

## Reports

Open **Reports** at the bottom of the inbox's views rail.

- **Conversation volume.** New conversations over time — spot the spike after a release before it spikes you.

- **Median first response.** How long customers wait for a human answer. Median, not average, so one forgotten thread doesn't distort the story.

- **Median resolution.** How long conversations take to close.

- **CSAT.** The average satisfaction score across rated conversations.

## CSAT

Satisfaction ratings come from the customer, right where the conversation happened:

- **The prompt appears on resolve.** When an agent resolves a thread, the customer sees a short rating prompt inside that thread in the messenger — once per conversation, and answering it (or dismissing it) is the end of it.

- **Scores follow the contact.** Each conversation's score appears in the details rail alongside the contact's **average across all their rated conversations** — context for how this person's experience has been trending.

- **Scores roll up.** The organization-wide average lands in Reports.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  CSAT runs on ProductBridge's survey engine — the same one behind [Surveys](/core-concepts) — so responses are stored as survey data and no extra setup is needed.
</Callout>

<Expandable title="Troubleshooting" default-open="false">
  - **No SLA badge on a conversation that should have one.** Check the policy's scope — a priority-scoped policy only applies to conversations *with* that priority set.

  - **CSAT shows no data.** Scores only exist after agents resolve conversations and customers respond to the prompt; freshly enabled workspaces start empty.

  - **Median metrics look empty for a period.** Medians need at least one qualifying conversation in the window — first-response needs an agent reply, resolution needs a resolve.
</Expandable>
