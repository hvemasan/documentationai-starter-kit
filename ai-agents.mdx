## What the Hub Is For

The AI Agents hub is where you build the assistant that answers conversations in your [Support inbox](/support-chat/agent-inbox) and [Messenger](/support-chat/messenger). Four things make up an agent, and each has its own section in the hub:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The agent" icon="bot" title-type="p">
    Its name, avatar, tone, response length, and the free-text instructions that tune how it replies. Configure it under [**Agents**](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/support/workflows/agent/agents).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Its content" icon="book-open" title-type="p">
    What it is allowed to answer *from* — snippets, crawled website pages, uploaded files, Help Center articles, and video transcripts. Manage it under **Content**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Its tools" icon="wrench" title-type="p">
    Your own API endpoints, so the agent can look up an order or issue a refund mid-conversation. Configure them under [**Tools**](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/support/workflows/agent/tools).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Its deployment" icon="rocket" title-type="p">
    Whether it may answer customers on its own, and on which channels. Turn it on under [**Deploy**](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/support/workflows/agent/deploy).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  The order of the sidebar follows that order: build the agent, give it something to answer from, then put it in front of customers.
</Callout>

## Grounding

The agent answers from the content you give it. The rules that stop it inventing answers live in ProductBridge's backend, not in your configuration — nothing you type into an agent's instructions can remove them.

<Callout kind="tip">
  If the agent won't answer a question, that is usually a content gap rather than a configuration problem. Add a [snippet](/ai-agents/content#snippets) covering the question and it will pick it up within a few minutes.
</Callout>
