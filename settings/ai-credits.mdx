## Where to find it

<Card title="Open Billing & Plans" href="https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing" icon="credit-card" horizontal="true">
  Sidebar → your avatar (bottom-left) → **Billing**
</Card>

**Direct link:** [https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing)

**In-app path:** Sidebar → your avatar (bottom-left) → **Billing**

**Requires:** `billing:view` to see the page, `billing:manage` to change a plan or buy credits. Owners have both; Admins have `billing:view` only.

<Callout kind="info">
  Billing is **not** under Settings. It moved out to its own top-level page because it is a per-user, cross-organization concern — one balance covers every organization you own.
</Callout>

The page has three tabs:

| Tab | Direct link | What it holds |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| **Current Plan** | [`/dashboard/billing`](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing) | Your plan, renewal date, seat and usage meters, and the **AI credits available** tile |
| **Plans** | [`/dashboard/billing?tab=plans`](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing?tab=plans) | The full plan comparison table — toggle monthly/yearly with `?interval=monthly` |
| **Top up credits** | [`/dashboard/billing?tab=topup`](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing?tab=topup) | One-time credit packs. Only appears when top-up packs are available for your account |


## What AI Credits Are

AI credits meter your AI usage across ProductBridge. Every AI operation debits credits from your account, including:

- **Ask AI** — each message you send charges 1 credit
- **Feedback pipeline** — AI analysis of incoming feedback from integrations and the portal
- **AI Writer** — AI-assisted drafting
- **AI search** — semantic search over your feedback

<Callout kind="info">
  Credits belong to your **account**, not to a single organization. If you own multiple organizations, they all draw from the same credit balance.
</Callout>

## Where Credits Come From

Your spendable balance combines two pools:

| Pool | Source | Expiry |
|------|--------|--------|
| Monthly allocation | Granted by your plan each month | Resets every 30 days — unused credits do **not** roll over |
| Top-up balance | One-time credit packs you purchase | Never expires |

Top-up credits are only spent **after** your monthly allocation is exhausted, so purchased credits are never wasted.

Your available balance is: `remaining monthly allocation + top-up balance`.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Promotions can also grant **bonus credits**, shown separately on the credits tile. Unlike top-ups, bonus credits can carry an expiry date — the tile shows how many days remain.
</Callout>

## Check Your Balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing" icon="credit-card" title-type="p">
    Click your avatar in the bottom-left of the sidebar and choose **Billing**, or go straight to [https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the credits tile" icon="gauge" title-type="p">
    On the **Current Plan** tab, the **AI credits available** tile shows your headline spendable balance, broken down into the monthly, bonus, and top-up pools. The monthly pool resets at the end of your current 30-day period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Viewing the balance requires `billing:view` on your role. Buying credits or changing plan requires `billing:manage`, which only Owners have by default.
</Callout>

## View Usage History

Every credit charge is recorded — the operation type, credits charged (split by pool), the organization it ran for, and when it happened.

<Callout kind="info">
  The usage ledger is currently available through the admin API only (`GET /credits/usage`, filterable by organization, operation type, and date range). The Billing page shows your current balance, not the per-operation history. If you need a usage breakdown, contact `help@productbridge.io`.
</Callout>

## Buy More Credits

When you need more than your monthly allocation:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Top up credits tab" icon="package" title-type="p">
    Go to [https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing?tab=topup](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing?tab=topup). The tab lists every one-time pack available to you, each showing the credit amount, price, and price per credit.

    <Callout kind="info">
      The **Top up credits** tab only appears when top-up packs are configured for your account. If you don't see it, the packs aren't available — contact `help@productbridge.io`.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy a pack" icon="shopping-cart" title-type="p">
    Click **Buy credits** on a pack to open the secure checkout. Every pack is marked **Never expires**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the balance to update" icon="refresh-cw" title-type="p">
    Credits are added right after payment is confirmed. This can take a few seconds — reload [the Billing page](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing) if the balance hasn't updated yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Top-up credits never expire, and you can buy packs even without an active subscription.

## Running Out of Credits

When your balance reaches zero:

- AI features pause — [Ask AI](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/ask-ai) and other AI operations show an **Out of AI credits** prompt instead of running, with a shortcut to [buy more](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/billing?tab=topup)
- Everything else keeps working — feedback boards, roadmap, changelog, portal, and integrations are unaffected (only the AI analysis of incoming items waits)
- You can either buy a top-up pack immediately or wait for your monthly allocation to reset
