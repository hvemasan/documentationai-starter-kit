**In-app path:** Sidebar → **Settings** → Connect → **MCP Server**

**Requires:** `mcp:view` to see the credentials, `mcp:manage` to regenerate them.

<Callout kind="info">
  The MCP server is configured under **Settings**, not under Connect Sources — it exposes ProductBridge to an AI assistant rather than pulling feedback in from another tool.
</Callout>

## Why AI Assistants?

Treat ProductBridge as a tool your AI assistant can use. Instead of copy-pasting feedback into a chat, your assistant can:

- Search your feedback by topic ("what are customers asking about onboarding?")
- Pull the current roadmap and group it by status
- Create a feedback post or changelog entry from a conversation
- Look up customer segments and filter by them

AI assistants connect via the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — an open standard for letting LLMs call external tools. ProductBridge exposes 22 MCP tools covering feedback, roadmap, changelog, and the surrounding configuration.
