## Where to find it

<Card title="Open Connect Sources" href="https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/settings/integrations" icon="plug" horizontal="true">
  Sidebar → **Connect Sources**
</Card>

**Direct link:** [https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/settings/integrations](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/settings/integrations)

**In-app path:** Sidebar → **Connect Sources**

**Requires:** `integration:view` to browse, `integration:manage` to connect or disconnect.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Each integration has its own page at `/dashboard/settings/integrations/{code}` — for example [`/slack`](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/settings/integrations/slack), [`/intercom`](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/settings/integrations/intercom), or [`/jira`](https://app.productbridge.io/dashboard/settings/integrations/jira).
</Callout>

## Capture Feedback from Anywhere

Data & Enrichment integrations extend ProductBridge beyond your existing tools. Capture feedback directly from any webpage and bring in context that makes your feedback more actionable.

## Available Tools

  <Card title="Chrome Extension" icon="chrome" href="/integrations/data-and-enrichment/chrome-extension">

## How It Fits

Data & Enrichment tools complement your other integrations by adding context that communication channels and review platforms cannot capture on their own. Use the Chrome Extension to grab feedback from competitor reviews, social media posts, or internal tools that do not have a direct integration.

<Callout kind="info">
  All feedback captured through Data & Enrichment tools flows into [Feedback Intelligence](/core-concepts/feedback-intelligence) for automatic categorization and analysis, just like feedback from any other source.
</Callout>
