## Opening Analytics

Select any survey from the **Surveys** list to open its analytics dashboard. The header carries three controls:

- **Refresh** — reload the latest responses.
- **Share by link** — copy or open the survey's public link (see [Distribution](/core-concepts/surveys/distribution#share-by-link)).
- **Date range** — scope every metric to a window; it defaults to the last 90 days.

The layout adapts to the survey type, so you always see the metrics that make sense for the question you asked.

## NPS

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Score | The headline NPS from −100 to +100, with the change versus the prior period. |
| Responses | Total responses in the selected window. |
| Response rate | Share of shown surveys that were answered. |
| Composition | The split of promoters, passives, and detractors. |

Below the tiles, a **score distribution** histogram plots responses across 0–10, colored by band, and a **daily score trend** tracks the score over time.

## CSAT

CSAT mirrors NPS but reads in satisfaction language:

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Satisfied | Percentage of respondents who rated 4–5. |
| Responses | Total responses in the window. |
| Response rate | Share of shown surveys that were answered. |
| Composition | The split of satisfied, neutral, and dissatisfied. |

A score distribution histogram and daily trend accompany the tiles, banded the same way.

## CES

CES is average-based rather than banded:

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Avg rating | The mean effort score, shown out of the scale maximum. |
| Responses | Total responses in the window. |

An **effort distribution** histogram plots responses across the configured scale in a single neutral color — there are no promoter/detractor bands for effort.

## Poll

A poll shows a **Responses** tile plus a **Results** breakdown: each option with its vote count and percentage as a progress bar.

<Callout kind="info">
  Percentages are the share of respondents who picked each option. In a multiple-choice poll, respondents can pick more than one option, so the percentages can add up to more than 100%.
</Callout>

## Open feedback

Open feedback shows a **Responses** tile and a scrollable list of every answer as a readable quote, each tagged with the respondent and the date. Anonymous responses are labeled accordingly. The list loads more as you scroll.

## Recent Responses

For scored and poll surveys, a **Recent responses** table lists individual answers with their score or selected options, any follow-up comment, the respondent, and the date. NPS and CSAT rows also show the band each answer falls into.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Combine analytics with the **Advanced** routing options on NPS and CSAT — send detractors to a feedback board and nudge promoters to leave a review — so the numbers here turn directly into action. See [Creating a Survey](/core-concepts/surveys/creating-a-survey#advanced).
</Callout>
