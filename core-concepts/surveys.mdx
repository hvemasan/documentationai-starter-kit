## What Surveys Are

Surveys let you ask your users short, focused questions at exactly the right moment — right after they finish onboarding, close a support ticket, or use a new feature. Instead of a long questionnaire, each survey asks **one question** in a lightweight card that appears in your app, on your public portal, or on a shareable link.

Every response flows into ProductBridge alongside the rest of your feedback, so a low NPS score or an open-ended complaint becomes a trackable signal — not a number in a spreadsheet you never revisit.

<Callout kind="info">
  Surveys run on their own lightweight script, separate from the feedback widget. A surveys-only customer never ships the widget bundle, and vice versa.
</Callout>

## The Five Survey Types

ProductBridge ships five survey types built on one unified engine. Every type renders as the same single-question card and stores its answers in the same place — so you can mix and match without changing how you read the results.

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="NPS" icon="gauge" href="/core-concepts/surveys/survey-types#nps">
    The classic 0–10 "how likely are you to recommend us" question, scored into promoters, passives, and detractors.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CSAT" icon="smile" href="/core-concepts/surveys/survey-types#csat">
    A quick 1–5 satisfaction rating to capture how a specific interaction felt.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CES" icon="zap" href="/core-concepts/surveys/survey-types#ces">
    Customer Effort Score on an editable 1–7 scale — how hard was it to get something done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Poll" icon="list-checks" href="/core-concepts/surveys/survey-types#poll">
    A single multiple-choice question with single- or multi-select answers and a live tally.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open feedback" icon="message-square" href="/core-concepts/surveys/survey-types#open">
    A free-text prompt that collects open-ended feedback in the customer's own words.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How It Fits Together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create and configure" icon="pencil" title-type="h3">
    Pick a type, write your question, and set who sees it, when it fires, and how often it can reappear. See [Creating a Survey](/core-concepts/surveys/creating-a-survey).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the script" icon="code" title-type="h3">
    Add the surveys script to your app once. Published surveys reveal themselves whenever a visitor is eligible. See [Installation & SDK](/core-concepts/surveys/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribute" icon="share-2" title-type="h3">
    Reach users through your app, your public portal, or a shareable link. See [Distribution](/core-concepts/surveys/distribution).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure and act" icon="bar-chart-3" title-type="h3">
    Read per-type analytics, and route detractors to a feedback board or nudge promoters to leave a review. See [Analytics](/core-concepts/surveys/analytics).
  </Step>
</Steps>
